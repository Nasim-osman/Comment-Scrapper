🎓 Thesis & Academic Research Notes

This tool is designed to assist academic researchers, thesis students, and NLP practitioners in compiling multilingual social media datasets. Always follow ethical research guidelines, respect privacy, and anonymize user personal data in your final published thesis.

⚡ Supported Output Features

  • UTF-8 BOM CSV: Opens in Microsoft Excel with 100% correct Bengali script.
  • Formatted Excel: Styled headers, auto-fit columns, word-wrap enabled.
  • Language Tags: Pre-classified as Bengali (bn), English (en), or Mixed.
  • Text Normalizer: Zero-width characters & excessive whitespace cleaned.

Account Sessions & Authentication

Meta platforms (Facebook & Instagram) require login to view full comment threads. You can connect your browser session once, and it will be saved for scraping.

Facebook Not Connected

Clicking below will open a visible Chromium browser window. Log in to Facebook there once, and your session will be saved automatically.

Instagram Not Connected

Open a browser window to log in to Instagram, or paste your `sessionid` cookie.

Twitter / X Not Connected

Open a browser window to log in to Twitter/X, or paste your `auth_token` cookies.

Reddit API (Optional)

Reddit works out-of-the-box via browser automation. If you have an official Reddit App script, you can enter credentials here for higher speed.
